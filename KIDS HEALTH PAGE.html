The World Health Organization (WHO)

Protecting and improving the health of children is of fundamental importance. Over the past several decades, we have seen dramatic progress in improving the health and reducing the mortality rate of young children. Among other encouraging statistics, the number of children dying before the age of 5 was halved from 2000 to 2017, and more mothers and children are surviving today than ever before.
However, a great deal of work remains to further improve the health outcomes for children. The world is facing a double mandate. More than half of child deaths are due to conditions that could be easily prevented or treated given access to health care and improvements to their quality of life.
At the same time, children must also be given a stable environment in which to thrive, including good health and nutrition, protection from threats and access to opportunities to learn and grow. Investing in children is one of the most important things a society can do to build a better future.

Why Is A Childs Health Important?

Child health is the foundation of long-term well-being, directly determining a child’s physical, cognitive, and emotional development.
Good health supports brain growth and school readiness, while establishing lifelong habits that prevent chronic adult diseases.
A child's health is critical because it dictates their capacity to learn, play, and thrive.
Key reasons include:Optimal Brain Development: Proper nutrition and preventative care during the early years are vital for healthy brain development and academic success.
Disease Prevention: A robust immune system helps children fight off infections and prevents long-term conditions such as childhood obesity, diabetes, and cardiovascular issues.
Emotional and Social Growth: Physical and mental well-being allows children to successfully regulate their emotions, build resilience, and develop strong social skills.
Lifelong Habits: The early years create a crucial baseline; healthy habits taught in childhood naturally carry into adulthood.
